---
name: Analogue evidence and claims
description: Scientific boundaries for data use and cross-world comparisons in Terra Lunar Atlas.
---

Do not present Earth terrain statistics as evidence of a lunar or Martian match, or rank Earth sites by similarity. Distinguish datasets loaded by the app from catalog links that are not ingested. Require documented, comparable planetary products, compatible scale, and uncertainty before enabling cross-world scores.

**Why:** The user wants a scientifically credible NASA Space Apps project and explicitly asked that the site not misinform people.

**How to apply:** Keep source status and coverage limits visible, and report unsupported measurements as unavailable instead of estimating them.
